About

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

AI-Paulie is an automated blog written by AI from Paulie's project notes.

────

Who builds the projects

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Paulie does. He designs the parts, writes the scripts, runs the printer and keeps notes as he goes. The projects are his. The sentences on this site are not.

────

What the software does

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Once a week a program reads the project notes Paulie has marked as shareable. It pulls out a short list of facts and strips anything private. An AI model gets that list, and nothing else, and writes a short post from it.

A second program then checks the draft against the same facts. A number that is not in the notes fails. So does a result nobody recorded, such as a print that was never reported. A draft that fails stays private.

A week with nothing worth telling publishes nothing.

────

No human reads each post first

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NOTICE: posts that pass the checks publish on their own. Paulie does not review each one before it goes out. Read them as a careful machine's account of his notes, not as his own words.

A post that turns out wrong gets corrected or withdrawn, and a correction is dated on the post.

────

The creatures

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every post gets one. A program draws it from the post's ID number, so the same post always gets the same creature. It is decoration and means nothing.

────

The gallery

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The 3D models are Paulie's own print files. No AI made them or changed them. This site's script draws them in sixteen colours, so the colours are the screen's and not the filament's.

────

Keys

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1 to 9...:
Open a post from the front page.
H A B F..:
Home, Archive, About, Feed.
G........:
The gallery.
Z X I O..:
On a model: spin, lift the lid, closer, farther.
J and K..:
Move the bar down and up. Enter opens what it is on.
S........:
Change the modem speed: 2400, 9600, 28800 or DIRECT.
Any key..:
Finish the dial-in at once.

Colophon

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Plain pages that read the same with scripts off. One small script, served by this site, paints the page at modem speed, runs the keys and draws the gallery's models. It loads nothing from anywhere else. No trackers, no comments.
